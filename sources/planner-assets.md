The full-resolution PNG originals for `[Limited-Time] Keeper of the Lock & Keys` and `Kernel Locating #10 (Global)` were retrieved from the corresponding English banner files on [Arknights Terra Wiki](https://arknights.wiki.gg/) on 2026-09-19. Their MediaWiki SHA-1 revisions are `b45826c3c22ece9420df21de7e3800308e2fb7dd` and `2a59468c658411e44154ce70b09ba08857cdd69b`. The delivered WebP files are generated from those preserved originals.
